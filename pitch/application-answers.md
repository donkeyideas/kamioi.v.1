### Business Model
```
Kamioi is a subscription-based micro-investing platform. Revenue comes from monthly plans: Individual ($9.99/mo), Family ($19.99/mo), and Business ($49.99/mo) — with yearly plans at ~17% discount. Each plan includes automatic round-up investing, AI-powered merchant-to-stock matching, portfolio tracking, and smart receipt scanning.

What we sell: Automated investing software that turns daily purchases into fractional stock investments matched to the brands consumers buy.

Who we sell to: Everyday consumers (18–45) who want to invest but find traditional platforms intimidating. Secondary markets include families managing joint investment goals and small businesses offering investment perks to employees.

How revenue comes in: Recurring monthly subscriptions. At scale, additional revenue from premium AI features, data insights, and potential B2B white-label licensing.

Unit economics: Near-zero marginal cost per user (cloud infrastructure + AI API calls ~$0.02/transaction). At ~$10/mo average subscription, breakeven at ~500 users with current burn rate.

The entire platform — web app, mobile app, admin dashboard, AI engine, receipt scanner — is built and functional. The only remaining piece is securing a Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) or Broker-Dealer partnership to process fractional share trades through Alpaca's API.
```
